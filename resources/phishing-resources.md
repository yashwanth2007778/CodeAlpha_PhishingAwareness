# Phishing Awareness Resources

## Purpose

These resources provide additional information about phishing, social engineering, and safe online practices.

## Recommended Resources

- CISA — Phishing Guidance
- FTC — How to Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams
- Google Safety Center — Phishing and Suspicious Messages
- Microsoft Security — Protecting Against Phishing

## Key Safety Practices

- Verify unexpected messages before taking action.
- Check website domains carefully.
- Never share passwords, PINs, or OTPs.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.
